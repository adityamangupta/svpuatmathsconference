About the conference

The conference bridges theoretical mathematics, allied sciences and their practical use in agriculture, science and technology. Faculty members, research scholars and students will see how mathematical modeling and advances in allied sciences solve complex problems in agriculture, environmental science, engineering and technology.

What you will gain

1. Forecasting models

Applied mathematics for weather prediction and agricultural planning.

2. Mathematical biology

Ecosystem models for biodiversity and conservation.

3. Optimization

Drug kinetics, treatment optimization, soil data analysis and irrigation scheduling.

4. Research trends

New opportunities at the interface of mathematics and applied sciences.

5. Sustainable energy

Advanced materials for solar energy conversion and sustainable technology.

About SVPUAT

Established on 2 October 2000, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel University of Agriculture & Technology is called the “First Agriculture University of the 21st century”, accredited Grade “A” by NAAC, with seven constituent colleges. It integrates education, research and extension to serve rural Uttar Pradesh, and is located at Modipuram on the Delhi–Dehradun highway, about 12 km from Meerut.

Faculty of Mathematics

The Faculty of Mathematics & Allied Sciences builds the foundation in basic and applied sciences that underpins teaching and research across agriculture, allied sciences and technology, delivering undergraduate and postgraduate instruction.
